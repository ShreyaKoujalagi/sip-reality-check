        WHY THIS EXISTS

        Your SIP. Your choice.

        A disappointing return can raise difficult questions. Reality Check helps make those questions easier to understand. It never tells you to continue, change, or stop investing.

        Concept prototype for user research · Not affiliated with GrowwAI-generated reflection may be incomplete

        About your information

        This prototype does not create accounts, store your answers in a database, or save a conversation history. Your text is sent through a Netlify server function to the Google Gemini API to generate the result. Your request is processed by those services under their applicable terms and policies. Avoid sharing account numbers, PAN details, or other sensitive information.

        The app does not access Groww, verify market data, or give personalized investment advice. The safety check can miss errors, so treat the reflection as a starting point—not a recommendation.
