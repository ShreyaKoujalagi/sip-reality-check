          WHY THIS EXISTS

          Your SIP. Your choice.

          A disappointing return can raise difficult questions. Reality Check helps make those questions easier to understand. It never tells you to continue, change, or stop investing.

          Concept prototype for user research · Not affiliated with GrowwAI-generated reflection may be incomplete

          About your information

          This prototype does not create accounts, store responses in its own database, or save a conversation history. If you opt in, only your starting point is saved in this browser’s local device storage. You can edit or delete it anytime. Browser data may be shared with other people who use the same browser profile, and clearing browser data removes it. Disabling this feature does not prevent provider-side processing of a submitted request. Your text is sent through a Netlify server function to the Google Gemini API to generate the result. Your request is processed by those services under their applicable terms and policies. Avoid sharing account numbers, PAN details, or other sensitive information.

          The app does not access Groww, verify market data, or give personalized investment advice. The safety check can miss errors, so treat the reflection as a starting point—not a recommendation.
